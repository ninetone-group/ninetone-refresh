## Method

One FileMaker Data API session (closed at the end) and bearer GETs on the Office website API. Every body is the one the getter in src/lib/ninetone.ts sends, including sort, limit, offset and portal limits. No cache layer sits in between, so both answers are live. Values are compared as strings after `String()`; keys and values are compared in Unicode NFC. Images are compared by state (URL present / empty / error), never by bytes.

Dependent finds follow the pages: `getWebPosts(category)` for every section the site asks for (homepage, three divisions, team, Promo, Guider, and `FAQ <tag>` for every booking category on either side), a per-slug `API_Booking` find for curated talents outside the strict roster, and a per-slug `API_ARTIST_DETAIL` find with the release portal for every active artist on either side.

Fields the site reads per layout are listed in `USED_FIELDS` in the script, taken from docs/handoff-codex-database-migration-2026-10-26.md §4 and checked against the pages with grep. A FileMaker field absent from that list is reported under "Fields the site never reads" rather than as a content difference.
